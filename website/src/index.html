08 Common questions

Clear answers
for a research build.

Scope is part of the product. Here is what Limulator needs and what its current evidence means.

Does this replace Xcode or the iOS Simulator?+

No. Limulator uses xtool and an Xcode SDK archive to build an iPhone app, then runs that app inside a locally emulated iOS guest. It is a Linux development and testing path with different tradeoffs from Apple's tools.

Is the native app workflow already proven?+

The 26-step compatible-graphics CounterLab smoke passed on one iPhone 16 guest. One closed-Duo BruTV session rendered its local H.264 HLS fixture; physical Play restored video 3.28 seconds after a 666.58-second pause, and source counters advanced in later captures. That is one recovery case, not proof of broad playback reliability or app performance. The iPad's native display classification now matches its profile after cache refresh and reboot. DataMigrator success: false still blocks clean creation; repeatable cold startup and full profile acceptance remain pending.

Can I use any iPhone, iPad, or App Store app?+

Compatibility is profile-specific. A closed-Duo session passed a narrow BruTV fixture playback and long-pause recovery trial; full posture, orientation, repeatable startup, and broad playback checks remain pending. The iPad's framebuffer density and ProgressUI screen class now match its profile in a preserved guest, but fresh creation, migration success, and a CounterLab .pad scenario remain unaccepted. Locally built ARM64 apps are the intended input; encrypted App Store executables are rejected.

Does a passing QEMU benchmark mean my app will run faster?+

No. The published measurement isolates one translated-code cache invalidation path. App boot, interaction, graphics and end-to-end latency have not been measured.

What does the public preview include?+

The public preview includes an iPhone CounterLab smoke, one closed-Duo fixture playback and recovery trial, and partial iPad display-classification recovery. These do not establish repeatable cold startup, general app performance, or complete device acceptance. DataMigrator success: false still blocks clean creation.
